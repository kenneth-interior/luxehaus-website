About Luxe Haus Concept

Thoughtful interior design and renovation solutions crafted for modern Singapore homes.

Our Story

Luxe Haus Concept was founded with a simple belief: a well-designed home should feel both beautiful and functional. We work closely with homeowners to create spaces that reflect their lifestyle, maximise comfort, and stand the test of time.

From BTO apartments and resale HDB flats to condominiums and landed properties, we provide interior design, renovation, and custom carpentry solutions tailored to each client’s needs.

Our Approach

We focus on thoughtful space planning, quality materials, practical storage solutions, and refined detailing. Every project is approached with transparency, care, and a commitment to delivering a smooth renovation experience from concept to completion.
